# Grand Arena Card Crafting System

## Crafting Requirements (from YouTube video: "How to Craft Cards in Moku Grand Arena")
- **3 Basic cards → 1 Rare card**
- **10 Rare cards → 1 Epic card**
- **8 Epic cards → 1 Legendary card**

## Key Rules
- Crafting is FREE (no gem cost, only consumes the lower-rarity cards)
- Scheme Cards CANNOT be crafted
- Crafted cards have no special cosmetics or serialization

## Crafting Paths to Legendary

### Path 1: Buy Legendary directly
- Buy 1 Legendary card from Ronin Marketplace

### Path 2: Craft from Epic
- Buy 8 Epic cards → craft 1 Legendary
- Cost = 8 × Epic floor price

### Path 3: Craft from Rare
- Buy 80 Rare cards → craft 8 Epics → craft 1 Legendary
- Cost = 80 × Rare floor price

### Path 4: Craft from Basic
- Buy 240 Basic cards → craft 80 Rares → craft 8 Epics → craft 1 Legendary
- Wait: 3 Basic → 1 Rare, so 80 Rares needs 240 Basics
- Cost = 240 × Basic floor price

### Mixed paths
- If user already owns some cards of a champion at various rarities, 
  subtract those from the requirements
